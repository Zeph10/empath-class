# Live Foundry acceptance test — v14.367 / D&D5e 6.0.5

These checks must be run in a disposable test world. They were not executed on a live Foundry server during this build.

1. Install the folder, enable only this module, reload, and inspect the browser console for module errors. Confirm three compendiums are visible and the macro opens the manager.
2. Create an Empath at level 1 through native class advancement. Check d6 HP, Wisdom/Charisma saves, disguise-tool proficiency and two chosen skills; no armor or weapon proficiencies. Increase to 3, select each Icon in turn on separate test actors, and confirm subclass features. Verify ASIs at 4/8/12/16/19, and features at 5/6/7/10/11/14/15/18/20. Repeat with Empath as a secondary multiclass.
3. Place a friendly Empath, one friendly ally and two hostile NPC tokens on a scene using feet. Form an allied link. Move the ally out of the 30-foot aura at level 1; link and depth must disappear. At level 5, a second allied link must be refused while a hostile second link is permitted.
4. Set Charisma 16 and raise to level 11. Confirm capacity 6, gain 2 and aura 45 feet. At level 7+, move a target out, accept Lingering Link, move it back before the Empath’s turn ends; link remains. Repeat without returning: it breaks at that turn end.
5. Start combat, advance the linked creature’s turn, and verify one save/voluntary-fail prompt per qualifying link. Voluntary failure gains depth to capacity; success does not. Verify hostile CR threshold and GM override; repeat with two GM clients and confirm only one prompts.
6. Apply Fervor to an ally with a native weapon/NPC weapon attack. Verify first weapon attack advantage, level-dependent crit range, first rolled weapon damage adds Charisma once, and next source turn start expires it. Apply Mantle for two rounds and check attack bonus / crit range / expiry. Apply stacked Apathy and verify suppression wins over every crit expansion; Resolve Damage removes its markers after one damage instance.
7. Terror: apply Terrorize. From the frightened NPC’s native damage card, apply damage to the Empath and verify no HP/temp HP loss. Apply damage to an unrelated ally and verify normal damage. Test forfeiting the turn and end-turn save. Test immunity changes at level 9, and conditional fear advantage in GM link controls.
8. Terror: test Fear of Failure with the linked ally and its victim selected. Confirm normal reroll, correct critical replacement damage and +40 psychic only on a second hit. Test Fear’s End self-attack with success/miss outcomes; no +100 on a miss. Test Intrusive Thoughts using Resolve Hit/Damage. Check Fear of Death doubles positive native-card damage once and leaves healing unchanged; compare with source-less manual damage, which the GM resolves.
9. Crowds: activate Valor, verify check d4, energize, then attack/save d4. Verify Trepidation d4 penalty, energized higher-of-two d4. Move friendly/hostile tokens in/out and confirm recipient markers follow dispositions. Sustain at Empath turn start; decline or exhaust depth and verify aura ends. Test one aura per link, Violence’s save/damage entry confirmation, repeated energize, and one-minute expiry. Confirm Fatigue’s late energize is refused.
10. Sorcery: discover **Spells 2024**, select two cantrips and two 1st-level spells at level 3. Verify excessive selections are rejected and missing names reported. Increase to 8/20 and verify table cantrip limits. Known spell copies must not alter the source compendium or other classes’ spells.
11. Sorcery: set one allied link to 4 depth and one hostile link to 3. Cast a level-3 spell using two allied depth and two hostile depth (6 energy). Check correct deduction and no spell slots consumed. Cancel before activation: depth and uses must remain unchanged. Test Body Fuel at level 9+, with correct energy reduction and unavoidable self-damage.
12. Sorcery: cast concentration spells on two different links. Apply damage to the Empath: no ordinary concentration save should be requested. Break one link: only its spell copy, tracked effects and templates end. Check target effects applied later via chat buttons carry module flags. Test replacing a concentration spell on one link preserves the other link and its unrelated ongoing spells. Instant spell copies remain usable for damage buttons and can be ended from the manager.
13. Verify Meeting of Minds transfers the same depth to the new token, consumes its short-rest use, clears old participant effects and blocks mood manipulations until the next source turn ends. Test short-rest recovery. Test Aura Max charge/recovery on long rest.
14. Cloud Mind: request saves, confirm observer-specific markers (not global invisibility), class/spell activation cleanup, and 10-minute world-time expiry. Trauma Dump: check chosen charmed/petrified cleanup and one exhaustion reduction.
15. Connect an ordinary player. Confirm owned actor manager access, action requests to the GM, target preservation/restoration, and no visible GM edit/override buttons. Unowned actors must not appear. Turn prompts remain on the designated GM, without duplication.
16. Save/reload the world. Verify actor flags, link depth and held spells persist. Delete a linked token: its link cleans up. Check behavior before enabling other automation modules. If another module duplicates damage/roll changes, disable this module’s roll-modifier setting and resolve those changes through the table’s chosen automation.

Record browser-console errors, the feature name, actor level/Icon, and the exact click sequence when reporting an issue. Native spell or module-specific summons/environmental cleanup is not covered by generic link cleanup; verify those manually.
